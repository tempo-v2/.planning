### Fresh selection in schema 21

Queue adds the share of ready introductions to the saved daily balance at
each fresh selection. A threshold derived from the study date and remaining
ready introduction count varies the gap between introductions. Selecting an
introduction subtracts one from the balance. When only one group has work,
it can proceed and the balance clears. Titles, ranking source and plan
generation identity do not affect this decision when the ready totals
are the same.

Store reads the published generation’s due count and the bounded ready
introduction membership. Existing checks still validate the chosen head.
Fresh issuance and repeat filler reservation save the balance with the
slot commitment. A rolled-back reservation saves neither. Active attempt
resume, receipt replay and consumption of committed deck entries leave the
balance alone. Schema 21 derives counts from the existing due rows and
begins mixing the remaining fresh work with a zero balance.

One writer, at most 128 affected rows per background write turn, existing writer admission and fairness. Managed cutover preserves predecessor tables and active/committed work.
