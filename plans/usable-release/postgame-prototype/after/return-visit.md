## Appearance and empty state

The large board, warm neutral background and readable serif headings give the page a consistent identity. The selected move is clear against adjacent moves. At desktop height, the move list scrolls separately, and comparing candidates or previewing pushes later actions lower. An immediate first-entry screenshot caught the board still showing its starting arrangement while the move and highlight already indicated 3…Nd4. Later captures showed the correct pieces.

Empty Games names both missing inputs, offers Open repertoires and Choose a PGN, and explains what importing enables. Import PGN, Import or refresh games and Choose a PGN are competing entry points for a first visit.

Screenshots in this directory use the return- prefix. High confidence in these observed desktop outcomes, with no claim about other viewports, other people, or cross-browser persistence.
